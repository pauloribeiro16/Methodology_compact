# Architecture & Data Inventory

## 1. Technical Architecture

OmniBank Financial Systems S.A. is a large credit institution (5,000+ employees, >€1.5B revenue) operating under ECB/BaFin supervision in Germany. The company provides consumer credit, mobile banking, and an AI-driven credit scoring platform (OmniScore). The architecture is hybrid: an on-premise core banking mainframe (regulatory-mandated data residency) coupled with EU-region cloud services for digital channels, AI/ML, analytics, and regulatory reporting. All five EU regulations apply (GDPR, CRA, NIS 2, DORA, AI Act), making OmniBank a DORA Financial Entity, NIS 2 Essential Entity, CRA mobile-app Manufacturer (Default Class), AI Act Annex III Provider+Deployer, and GDPR Controller+Processor. Maturity is high-tier: ISO 27001 certified + documented third-party security attestation for payment-card scope, 100-person security organisation, in-house 24/7 SOC + CSIRT, dedicated CISO/DPO/CRO/AI Governance Lead, ECB-supervised.

### 1.2 Network Topology

OmniBank operates a **three-zone architecture**: (1) **Production on-premise** (CBS mainframe, payments, treasury, cards, loan systems) hosted across EU data centres with synchronous replication and disaster-recovery site; (2) **EU cloud production** (digital channels, AI/ML, analytics, regulatory reporting) under DORA-compliant multi-supplier contract; (3) **Security zone** with HSM cluster (SYS-22), SIEM (SYS-23), and SOC tooling (SYS-25) in segregated network + on-prem with physical dual-control access. Branch network (SYS-19) connects to CBS via IPSec VPN over EU carrier MPLS. Card management (SYS-05) sits in card-scheme security-attestation scope, isolated via firewall segmentation from other zones. SWIFT (SYS-06) is logically and physically segregated per SWIFT CSP 2024 controls. Open banking APIs (SYS-18) terminate in a DMZ with PSD2-compliant eIDAS-qualified certificates. Inter-zone traffic uses mutual transport cryptographic authentication over modern transport cryptographic standard; intra-zone traffic uses internal mutual cryptographic authentication or dedicated high-speed channels. Production on-prem and EU cloud are connected via redundant IPSec tunnels with explicit named flows documented in §2.2.

The architecture intentionally aligns **regulatory data residency** (BaFin/ECB data must stay in EU) with **DORA Art. 24 digital operational resilience testing** (annual TLPT for the CBS mainframe + OmniScore AI) and **AI Act Art. 9 risk-management system** for the OmniScore high-risk AI.
