- Two P7 recommendations: (a) expand GAP-002 reference to 4 subdomains → emit 4 FLAGS, OR (b) add a Domain-typed FLAGS variant. Default: GAP-002 has zero FLAGS edges, audit documents the gap.

This is the exact behaviour the brief asked the validator to **confirm**. **PASS.**

## 11. Top-3 findings

1. **GAP-002 / D-01 ontology gap is real and intentional** — flagged via CFL-005. The graph correctly **stores** the verbatim `affected_subdomain_ids=["D-01"]` (faithful to source) and **does not** emit a phantom FLAGS edge (which would either violate `id_patterns` or require fabricating a fake `D-01` SecurityControlDomain node). Resolution is a human (P7) decision on either expanding the gap to 4 subdomains or adding a Domain-typed FLAGS variant. This is the right design for a methodology artifact.

2. **Doc03 §4 BG Owner/Affected labels exceed the 7-stakeholder register** — `Lead Dev` (BG-02, BG-04) and `Procurement` (BG-05) have no STK-ID. The executor correctly chose **conservative 1-step-unambiguous mapping** (only CTO/CUSTOMER/DPO/CEO mapped) per the brief's "if inferred, NOT ADDED as an edge" rule. The 3 unmapped edges are documented in CVG-005 for Phase 2 follow-up. This means Phase 2 RACI work will see CTO/CUSTOMER as the only owners of BG-02/04/05 and may misallocate work — **a downstream consumer warning is appropriate**.

3. **Stricter stakeholder mapping could surface the Doc03 register gap** — Doc03 §3.1 has only 7 rows despite Doc03 §4 BG-02 alone listing 4 distinct roles (CTO, Lead Dev, B2B clients, EU market-surveillance authorities). BAM-003 captures the EU-authorities gap and CVG-005 captures the Lead Dev/Procurement gap. Both are low-severity today but compound for Phase 2. **Recommended Phase 2 action**: extend Doc03 §3.1 from 7 to 10 stakeholders (add `STK-LEADDEV-01`, `STK-PROCUREMENT-01`, `STK-AUTHORITY-EU-01`) and re-emit 4 more DEFINES edges + 1 SUPPORTS edge — total extension ~5 nodes, ~5 links.
