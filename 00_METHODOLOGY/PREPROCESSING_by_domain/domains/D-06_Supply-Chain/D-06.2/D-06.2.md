specification of the format and elements of the SBOM — a forward-

#### D-06.2 Software Bill of Materials (SBOM)

<!-- participants: CRA (sole authority) -->

**Participants (from CRDA):** CRA (sole authority)

**Sole-authority note:** GDPR, NIS2, DORA, and AI Act are all out-of-scope for D-06.2 per AEGIS taxonomy §4.1. CRA Annex I Part II (1) is the SOLE AUTHORITY for SBOM. This is the only sub-domain in Part C where CRA is the sole authority across all 5 regulations.

**Pairwise matrix:** No applicable pairs — only one participant (CRA).

**NIST CSF 2.0 anchoring via ID.AM-02** (Inventories of software platforms and applications). CRA maps ID.AM-02 via SR-CRA-008 (machine-readable format) and SR-CRA-052 (top-level dependencies) and SR-CRA-053 (technical documentation). Single-regulation; CSF mapping is consistent. The CRA Annex II §9 SBOM access location (optional user access) is not separately mapped by CSF 2.0; it is a product-instruction construct.

**Downstream implication (Phase 1C Doc 07):** Phase 2 obligation derivation produces a single-track SBOM obligation per CRA. Phase 3 case generation must validate that CRA-classified case companies produce and maintain an SBOM covering top-level dependencies in a commonly used machine-readable format (e.g., SPDX or CycloneDX). The Annex VII §8 MSA-disclosure obligation is an institutional hook, not an operational requirement; case companies do not need to provide the SBOM to users by default (Annex II §9 is optional).

> **Pattern.** Each Part quoted verbatim with highlighting, then per-clause analysis. Universal `[recital NN]` convention.

### 1. Annex I — full text

#### 3.7 CRA-CL143 — Part II (1) — SBOM

**Source locus:**

> identify and document vulnerabilities and components contained in products with digital elements, including by drawing up a **software bill of materials** in a commonly used and machine-readable format covering at the very least the **top-level dependencies** of the products;

**Instance 1 — VAG — S3 — `commonly used and machine-readable format`**

`commonly used` — which formats?

- **R1:** CycloneDX (OWASP).
- **R2:** SPDX (Linux Foundation).
- **R3:** Any machine-readable format.

## CRA v0.2 — Annex VIII Conformity Assessment Procedures

> **Source.** OJ L, 20.11.2024, p. 75–80. `03_REFERENCE_MATERIAL/Regulatory_References/CRA.txt`, lines ~4426–4769.
>
> **Pattern.** Key clauses quoted, then per-clause analysis.
>
> **Annex VIII defines three conformity-assessment modules:** Module A (internal control), Module B+C (EU-type examination + internal production control), Module H (full quality assurance). Art. 32 routes products to modules based on classification.

---

### 1. Module architecture

| Module | Part | Name | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | I | Internal control | Manufacturer self-assessment. |
| **B** | II | EU-type examination | Notified body examines design + vuln handling. |
| **C** | III | Internal production control | Manufacturer controls production per EU-type. |
| **B+C** | II + III | EU-type + production control | Notified body examines design; manufacturer controls production. |
| **H** | IV | Full quality assurance | Notified body audits QMS. |
